description: Draw ASCII art of Scotty the Scottish Terrier, Carnegie Mellon's mascot
---

Draw an ASCII art picture of Scotty, the Scottish Terrier who is the mascot of
Carnegie Mellon University. Show a small, sturdy terrier in side profile with
pointy triangular ears, a long rectangular snout, short legs, and a jaunty
upright tail — the same kind of pose used on the classic Scottie dog
silhouette.

Requirements:
- Use only plain ASCII characters (no Unicode box-drawing or emoji).
- Keep the art wide enough to be recognizable, roughly 40-60 columns.
- Add the caption "GO SCOTS!" (or "Carnegie Mellon University") beneath the art.
- Respond with ONLY the ASCII art and caption — no extra commentary or
  explanation before or after it.
